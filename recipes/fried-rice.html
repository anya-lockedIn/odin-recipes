Fried Rice Recipe

Description

Ingredients

  • 2 chopped baby carrots
  • 1/2 a cup frozen green peas
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 cups cooked and chilled white rice
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoons sesame oil

Steps

  1. Place carrots in saucepan and cover with water. Bring to boil for 3 to 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in peas then immediately drain in a colander.
  3. Heat a wok over high heat then pour in vegetable oil.
  4. Stir in carrots, peas and garlic; cook for about 30 seconds.
  5. Add eggs; stir quickly to scramble eggs with vegetables.
  6. Stir in cooked rice.
  7. Add soy sauce and toss rice to coat.
  8. Drizzle with sesame oil and toss again.
  9. Serve hot and enjoy!
